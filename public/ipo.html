IPO Advisory Services

The decision to go public through an Initial Public Offering (IPO) is one of the most critical and defining milestones a business owner can face. Taking an enterprise public is an intricate, time-consuming process fraught with regulatory hurdles, market volatility, and rigorous compliance demands.

The myriad of issues that must be addressed to achieve a successful offering requires the involvement of an experienced, objective advisor. At Aakkam, we possess the breadth and depth of capital market expertise to guide business owners through every stage of this transformative journey.

Phase 1: IPO Readiness Assessment

Our team assists in determining whether an IPO represents the optimal step in your company's development. We evaluate corporate readiness across key operational dimensions:

  • Internal Controls & Governance: Auditing internal accounting systems, risk frameworks, and statutory reporting rigor.
  • Financial Information & Audits: Verifying historical financial statement availability, restatements, and audit qualifications.
  • Executive Compensation & ESOPs: Structuring appropriate compensation and long-term incentive plans to retain and motivate leadership in a public company environment.

If an assessment determines that an IPO is not currently the right step, our Strategy Group helps identify high-value alternatives, such as strategic private equity rounds, joint ventures, or merger opportunities.

Phase 2: Underwriter Selection & Lead Manager Syndication

Selecting the right investment bankers and merchant underwriters is paramount. We work with you to evaluate and select the best underwriters for your offering based upon:

Domain Track Record

Proven underwriting experience with similar sector and market capitalization peers.

Distribution Reach

Institutional book building, foreign portfolio investor (FPI) access, and retail network.

Commercial Terms

Optimal syndication cost, underwriting commissions, and alignment on valuation bands.

Phase 3: Registration, MD&A, and Regulatory Filing

We guide leadership throughout the formal registration and prospectus process. This includes drafting the Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP), crafting the required Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A), responding directly to regulatory comments (SEBI / SEC), and organizing analyst roadshows.

Phase 4: Post-Listing Compliance & Investor Relations

Once public, an enterprise faces continuous reporting responsibilities. Aakkam establishes the reporting infrastructure necessary for periodic financial disclosure, board committee compliance, and supplemental reporting for research analysts and institutional investors.
