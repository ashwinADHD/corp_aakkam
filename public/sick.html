Sick Industries Revival & Restructuring

We provide specialized advisory solutions to distressed and sick industrial enterprises to help them navigate difficult economic cycles and tide over challenging times. Depending on the unique requirements of the firm, we negotiate decisively on behalf of our clients with their Bankers and financial institutions for:

  • One Time Settlement (OTS): Negotiating favorable compromise settlements with lending institutions.
  • Restructuring & Revival Packages: Re-aligning debt amortizations and repayment schedules to cash-flow realities.
  • Extended Compliance Time: Securing additional runway for firms to meet institutional directives.
  • Statutory & Regulatory Relief: Maximizing protection and relief against coercive actions by regulatory and recovery tribunals (such as BIFR/IBC frameworks).
  • Fresh Capital Infusion: Assisting distressed entities in raising fresh capital from banks and private investors to chart a viable path forward.

1. Financial Restructuring

Fresh investments are arranged in the form of equity participation, term loans, non-plan assistance, or comprehensive revival packages that involve the strategic write-off of past losses and infusion of working capital.

Key measures orchestrated by Aakkam include:

Interest & Penal Waivers

Advocating for waiver of compound interest, penal interest, and associated processing charges.

Debt-to-Equity Conversions

Converting unmanageable loan burdens into equity or preference shares to de-leverage balance sheets.

Payment Moratoriums

Securing multi-year moratoriums on principal and interest repayments during operational turnaround.

Guarantees & Collateral Restructuring

Realigning corporate and government guarantees to restore institutional lending confidence.

2. Business Restructuring

Reviving an enterprise requires revitalizing the underlying operating model. Our advisory practice coordinates operational transformations tailored to company requirements:

  • Management Realignment: Bringing in seasoned operational leadership and governance controls.
  • Hiving Off Viable Units: Carving out productive enterprise divisions into separate viable entities.
  • Closure of Unviable Divisions: Rationalizing chronic loss-making lines to preserve working capital.
  • Strategic Joint Ventures: Inducting partners capable of injecting technical, financial, and marketing capabilities.
  • Product Mix & Marketing Optimization: Re-orienting the commercial strategy to high-margin demand segments.
