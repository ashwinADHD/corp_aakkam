About Our Firm

Aakkam Corporate Solutions is a financial consulting and advisory firm started with the vision to make one of the most efficient financial and advisory services hubs with qualitative human interfacing technology.

The mission of the company is maximizing client satisfaction through innovative, transparent, and need-based solutions. Aakkam is dedicated to providing top-notch financial solutions packages to its clients to help them achieve and sustain competitive advantage in the industry.

Strategic Advisory in High-Growth Markets

We provide end-to-end advisory solutions to companies in high-growth markets for their capitalization and recapitalization strategy. Our mission is to help our clients secure the desired funding in the most efficient and time-bound manner. We not only provide advisory services to our clients but also make sure that the funding we arrange for our clients fits seamlessly into their long-term growth strategy. We provide our clients with a host of debt and equity solutions tailored to meet their exact funding requirements.

“During the course of our work, we have developed close interpersonal relationships with various Bankers & Private Equity firms, ensuring our clients’ mandates get executed in the most desirable manner within a legitimate time span.” Aakkam Corporate Leadership

Our Ethos and Multidisciplinary Team

Our team of experienced professionals is dedicated to providing outstanding service, quantifiable value, and creative solutions that maximize gain for our clients. Our ethos and business approach are based upon complete trust, transparency, and relentless focus on client success.

Key Areas of Practice

  • Fund Raising Solutions: Term loans, equity placement, and securitization.
  • Sick Industries Revival & Restructuring: Turnaround advisory and OTS bank settlements.
  • Project Preparation & Appraisal: Comprehensive feasibility studies, financial modeling, and DPR.
  • Due Diligence: Thorough business and legal diligence for acquisitions and private equity.
  • M & A Advisory: Takeovers, inorganic growth strategy, and fair valuations.
  • IPO Advisory: Comprehensive readiness and registration support for going public.
  • Venture Capital & Tax Planning: Growth-stage financing and strategic corporate tax structures.
