M & A Advisory Solutions

High levels of competitive pressure and an increasing appetite for accelerated growth have led forward-thinking firms across geographies and sectors to pursue inorganic expansion. Mergers, Acquisitions, and Takeovers provide a robust growth vehicle ideally suited for enterprises seeking swift entry into new markets, expanded geographic reach, strategic product categories, or a broader institutional customer base.

In India, corporate houses have experienced rapid acceleration in corporate restructuring driven by:

  • Economic Liberalization & Regulatory De-shackling: The deshackling of restrictive regulatory provisions and modern relicensing policies.
  • Intensified Competition: Surging domestic and global competition necessitating scale and operating efficiencies.
  • Mounting Margin Pressures: Demand for larger transaction volumes prompting strategic mergers or demergers of non-core entities.
  • Resource Optimization: Comprehensive restructuring to streamline operational profitability and maintain industry competitiveness.

Specialized Mergers & Takeovers Team

The specialized Merger and Acquisition team at Aakkam—through their deep understanding of Indian capital markets, regulatory bodies (SEBI, MCA, NCLT), relationships with financial intermediaries, and research orientation—has established a unique knowledge infrastructure to help enterprises plan, design, and execute aggressive growth strategies.
