Due Diligence Services

We conduct comprehensive due diligence on acquisition targets, private equity investments, and joint venture opportunities. We review the entire business—its dynamics, operating processes, and supply chains as well as the balance sheet figures—because the critical risks inherent in corporate acquisitions rarely appear in the numbers alone.

We create tangible value by delivering actionable opinions and practical advice, not merely confirming what you already know.

“Before investing in a company, our rigorous review ensures there are no skeletons in the corporate closet and confirms that perceived market opportunities are genuine.” Aakkam Due Diligence Practice

Our due diligence process focuses on two essential pillars: Business Diligence and Legal Diligence.

1. Business Due Diligence

Business due diligence accomplishes several pivotal objectives:

  • Product & Market Validation: Independently confirming assertions regarding the target company's product, service differentiation, and addressable market.
  • Competitive & Financial Stress-Testing: Rigorous analysis to gauge the risk-reward profile and determine true return on investment.
  • Technology & Intellectual Property Audit: Assessing proprietary software, patents, hardware designs, and verifying freedom-to-operate against infringement risks.
  • Stakeholder Reference Checks: Engaging key enterprise customers, suppliers, and industry specialists to assess client retention and supplier reliability.
  • Management Appraisal: Thorough background evaluation of founders and key executives, judging leadership culture, decision-making, and problem-solving resilience.

2. Legal & Corporate Document Checklist

Our legal and compliance specialists conduct an exhaustive audit of core enterprise documents, including:

Key Contracts

Customer agreements, master service agreements, and vendor contracts.

Employment Agreements

Key executive retainers, non-compete clauses, and IP assignment covenants.

Governance Records

Board and shareholder meeting minutes, resolutions, and statutory filings.

Corporate Charters

Articles of Association, Memorandum of Association, and company bylaws.

Litigation Records

Active and past disputes, regulatory notices, and contingent liabilities.

Financing Agreements

Past cap tables, shareholder pacts, debt covenants, and pledge agreements.
