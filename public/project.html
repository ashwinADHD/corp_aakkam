Project Preparation & Appraisal

Though every business is ultimately run on the ground, it is the strength of the financial documentation and its structured presentation that enables investors and bankers to make confident decisions regarding extending capital exposure to a firm.

We undertake comprehensive feasibility studies of new capital projects for our clients by discounting future cash flows and advising on overall investment viability. If an initiative exhibits a negative Net Present Value (NPV), we diagnose and recommend remedial structural modifications to restore commercial sustainability.

Our team provides complete preparation of Detailed Project Reports (DPR). Our proprietary financial modeling tools and templates, coupled with extensive institutional experience, ensure our project reports stand out among peers in bank appraisals.

Strategic Inquiries Prior to Project Formulation

Before formulating a project report, our advisory team interrogates six fundamental vectors:

What For

Clarifying core objectives, investment targets, and intended returns.

How

Defining operational processes, technologies, and internal/external resources.

Who

Identifying project partners, management key persons, and stakeholders.

When

Establishing realistic milestone schedules, gestation periods, and runway.

Where

Analyzing physical geography, supply chains, logistics, and location advantages.

What

Detailing core activities, product lines, and operational workflows.

Aakkam's Systematic Project Preparation Methodology

1. Project Need Analysis

We analyze four critical pillars: the problem, solutions, beneficiaries, and decisions. The problem identifies the catalyst requiring intervention, solutions address the root challenge, and beneficiaries define commercial viability. The decision to undertake capital expenditure hinges on the synthesis of these three factors.

2. Problem Formulation & Scoping

We conduct thorough market and technical research to define the project scope, intervention areas, and expected outcomes. Macro-level and micro-level objectives are decoupled to prevent capital misallocation.

3. Project Planning & Activity Mapping

Aakkam prepares exhaustive activity charts consisting of start dates, project calendars, milestones, evaluation periods, slack time allocations, and designated coordination managers.

4. Project Budgeting

We prepare two distinct budgetary architectures:

  • Cost Category Budget: Capital expenditures, equipment, materials, and administration.
  • Activity Budget: Real-time operational cost per output, factoring in contingencies and multi-year inflation assumptions.

5. Multi-Dimensional Feasibility Appraisal

Our comprehensive feasibility audits examine five rigorous appraisal dimensions:

Management Appraisal

Leadership competence, track record, and execution capability.

Technical Feasibility

Process engineering, technology sourcing, and input availability.

Financial Feasibility

NPV, IRR, DSCR, sensitivity analysis, and break-even points.

Commercial Appraisal

Market demand sizing, competitive landscape, and pricing power.

Economic Appraisal

Social cost-benefit metrics and macroeconomic viability.

6. Implementation & Operational Rollout

Sequencing operational activities, establishing functional dependencies, and supervising early-stage capital deployment.

7. Monitoring and Periodical Evaluation

Tracking resource utilization, identifying deviations from planned timelines, and providing corrective intervention before milestones are compromised.
