Corporate Tax Planning & Advisory

In a dynamic regulatory environment, forward-looking tax planning is an indispensable driver of enterprise value, shareholder returns, and capital preservation. Proactive fiscal structuring enables companies to optimize cash flow, eliminate double taxation, and maintain seamless compliance with fiscal authorities.

Aakkam Corporate Solutions provides comprehensive direct and indirect tax advisory services. Our multidisciplinary team of corporate tax strategists, chartered accountants, and legal advisors works closely with corporate boards to engineer robust, compliant tax frameworks tailored to your operating model.

“Effective tax planning is not merely an annual compliance exercise—it is a continuous strategic advantage in corporate capital allocation.” Aakkam Tax Advisory Desk

Our Core Tax Planning Solutions

Transaction Tax Structuring

Optimizing tax exposure during mergers, acquisitions, hive-offs, slump sales, demergers, and capital reorganizations.

Corporate Direct Tax Planning

Maximizing allowable exemptions, capital allowances, depreciation optimization, and R&D incentives under prevailing tax codes.

Indirect Taxation & GST Advisory

Structuring supply chains, input tax credit (ITC) optimization, customs classifications, and GST compliance reviews.

Transfer Pricing & Cross-Border Tax

Benchmarking arm's-length international transactions, Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements (DTAA), and master file documentation.

Dispute Prevention & Revenue Representation

In addition to strategic planning, Aakkam assists clients in navigating tax assessments, responding to show-cause notices, securing advance rulings, and representing corporate interests before appellate tribunals.

  • Advance Tax & Withholding Strategies: Formulating accurate quarterly cash projections and TDS/TCS mechanisms.
  • Turnaround Tax Relief: Leveraging accumulated business losses and unabsorbed depreciation in distressed asset revivals.
  • Executive & Shareholder Taxation: Efficient structures for ESOPs, promoter repatriation, and capital gains planning.
