Fund Raising Solutions

India is increasingly gaining global importance as a major market hub for investments from all over the world. In this fast-growing market, the thrust is on making the right move at the right time. When the stakes are high, you need a partner who understands your needs and the critical importance of transactions being undertaken.

Our Investment Banking division is dedicated to providing entrepreneurs and investors end-to-end services for identifying avenues for growth (organic or otherwise), helping forge strategic alliances and joint ventures, and cultivating and concluding acquisition/divestment transactions through aggressive negotiation skills and a result-oriented approach. We aim to create significant value for entrepreneurs and companies by helping them execute the right strategy. We have focused on mid-size clients who require more than just advice; we believe the best solution for the client lies in a blend of various services provided at a single point.

At Aakkam, we possess the necessary knowledge and relationships to access private capital for expansion, recapitalization, shareholder liquidity events, leveraged buyouts, acquisitions, and debt financing through high-quality institutional, financial, and strategic investors. We serve as objective advisors presenting a broad range of financing options to ensure alignment with each client's specific objectives and strategies.

Our professionals have the transaction experience, financial acumen, and capital source relationships needed to deliver superior advice and execution. We handle every facet of your company's capitalization to gain maximum flexibility, competitive pricing, and attractive terms.
